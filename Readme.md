“Between Life and Loss” is a story-time narrative game where every choice carries weight. The player is the only medic on a distant island. One day, strangers in red suits arrive and take some of people away. Days later, they return a few but none survive more than a week. Soon after, the rest of the village begins to fall ill. A deadly virus spreads across the island. Fear rises, because no one knows the cure except the player. As the only healer, the player is the island’s last hope. But the cure you create is limited. You must search for ingredients, craft the medicine, and decide who to save. Will the player protect his/her family, or sacrifice for the greater good of the village?

Every decision comes with regret. "Every life saved means another life lost." In the end, sacrifices must be made.
